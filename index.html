About Organ Donation

Organ donation is the process of giving an organ to help a person who needs a transplant.

One donor may be able to help multiple people. Organ donation is an important way of helping others and creating awareness.

Why Donate?

  • Help save lives
  • Give hope to patients
  • Support families
  • Create awareness

Organs That Can Be Donated

❤️ Heart

Helps patients with serious heart conditions.

🫘 Kidney

Kidneys are among the most commonly transplanted organs.

🫁 Lungs

Lung transplantation can help patients with severe lung disease.

🧬 Liver

Liver transplantation can help patients with liver failure.
